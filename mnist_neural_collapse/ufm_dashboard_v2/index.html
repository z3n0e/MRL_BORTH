What the experiment shows

The dashboard is organized around the main empirical claim: vanilla MRL-UFM reaches perfect classification but converges to a low-rank, rank-saturated nested geometry. Block-wise variance regularization reactivates dead coordinate blocks and recovers most of the NC geometry.
